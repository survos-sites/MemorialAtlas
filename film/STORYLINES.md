# Storylines

## A. The Sorting Machine (picked)

Device: the object is the explanation. Form: one frame that rebuilds, with object-to-object handoffs.
One real photo (Hamilton, St. Peter's) travels through a machine carved into granite: OCR boxes
light on the letters, brass signals run through Jev's decision gates with their real probabilities,
a switch sends the photo to the vision route and to cleanup, and the result lands as a real record.
Other real photos race through the same machine and take their own routes.

- First two seconds: the Hamilton phone photo fills the frame, crooked and sunlit; the word
  "Hamilton" gets a yellow OCR box before anything else moves.
- Beats: hook (one photo) → montage (111 photos, one cold afternoon) → the hard photos → reveal
  (MemorialAtlas: Read · Decide · Clean up) → Read → Decide → Clean up → the record, the site, MCP → end card.
- Look: polished dark granite ground, carved Roman capitals for titles, brass for the signal, the
  site's OCR yellow for boxes.
- Sound: a steady low pulse that builds through the machine; soft key ticks as OCR lines land,
  a click on each gate, one riser into the cleaned photo.

## B. One stone, three readers

Device: the result first. Form: one continuous take.
The finished record plays first, then the camera pulls back through the three readers (OCR, Jev,
Gemini) to the crooked phone photo it came from.

- Beats: payoff → pull back through cleanup → decision → OCR → the photo → end card.
- Look: the site's own paper and green; photos full bleed.
- Sound: piano bed, reversed swells on each pull-back.

## C. The wall of 111

Device: a number lands. Form: split screen to one.
All 111 photos tile the frame; each flips to its record or its cleaned view as the pipeline reaches
it; the wall collapses into one record.

- Beats: the wall → flips by stage → counts → collapse to Hamilton → end card.
- Look: a contact sheet on a light-table white.
- Sound: cut on the beat, a tick per flip.

Picked A because the decision tree is the part only this project has, and one real photo carries it.
